# Rutgers Esports Club Website

Public marketing site for Rutgers Esports game communities and related org presence.

## Language

**Club**:
A student game community affiliated with Rutgers Esports that appears in Meet Our Clubs.
_Avoid_: Game club (as a distinct type), chapter, org (on the site surface)

**Social link**:
A public URL for a Club on a specific platform (Discord, Instagram, or X), shown as an icon (fade-in on hover; always visible on touch).
_Avoid_: Handle (when a full URL is required), getInvolved / CampusLabs link, Facebook/Twitch on Club cards

**Scarlet Smash Melee**:
The Melee Scarlet Smash Club (distinct from Scarlet Smash).
_Avoid_: Merging Melee into a single Smash card

**Scarlet Smash**:
The non-Melee Scarlet Smash Club (covers Ultimate / general Smash identity on the site).
_Avoid_: Scarlet Smash Ultimate (as a third Club), merging with Melee

**RFGC**:
The Rutgers Fighting Game Community Club; card title is **RFGC**.
_Avoid_: Fighting Games, Fighting Game Community (as the card title)
